Biblically Literate AI

AI is a theological question before it is a technical one. People bear God's image and carry a responsibility no system can hold for them.

Human Agency & Formation

"Did God really say?" was an attack on knowing before it was an attack on doing. The serpent's method — reshape the question, and the choice follows — is the same method a ranked feed uses on you before breakfast.

Half the questions in this guide lean on this one. Systems now draft before you write, rank before you read, and filter before you look. Understanding how choices get shaped before you make them is the literacy everything else builds on.

The spine of the whole guide

The law was never aiming at the hand alone. Neither is the machine.

Jesus moved the commandments from the visible act to the formation that precedes it — anger before murder, the look before adultery, the want before the taking. That is exactly where AI does its work. Ten commandments, two great commands, four pillars: the full mapping is one page.
