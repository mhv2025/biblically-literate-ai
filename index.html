A Field Guide for People of Faith

Biblically Literate AI

AI is a theological question before it is a technical one: people bear God's image and carry a responsibility no system can hold for them.

Practicing Christians now use AI more than the average American — and most say no one is guiding them. The questions these tools raise are not new. Scripture has been answering them for a very long time: what only a person can carry, what truth requires before you repeat it, what was entrusted to you, and what your work is for.

Twenty-one topics, ordered the way the life of faith is ordered:

Know God · Love God · Love People · Live Faithfully
